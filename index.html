⚠ CRITICAL NOTES
  • This tool is a preliminary sizing aid only. It does NOT replace a licensed Texas PE design or a site-specific geotechnical investigation.
  • Effective PI, slope correction, over-consolidation, and fill conditions must come from a soils report. Uncompacted fill under beams will cause failure.
  • Metal building column reactions (from the ACT/Mueller stamped drawings) must be checked separately for local bearing and uplift anchors.
  • Always provide both positive and negative beam steel and continuous slab reinforcing. Do not cut diaphragm sheeting.
How to Use This Tool
  1. Enter the overall building length and width in feet.
  2. Enter the Texas ZIP code. The tool estimates a Climatic Rating (Cw) based on broad regional bands.
    • Houston / Gulf Coast / East Texas (77xxx) ≈ 25
    • Dallas / North Central (75xxx–76xxx) ≈ 20–22
    • San Antonio / South Central (78xxx) ≈ 17–18
    • West Texas / Panhandle (79xxx) ≈ 15
    Override Cw directly if you have a site-specific value.
  3. Enter the Effective Plasticity Index from the geotechnical report (15-ft weighted average). This is the single most important soil parameter.
  4. Adjust unit weight if needed (200 psf is conservative for most 1-story brick-veneer or light metal buildings).
  5. Click Calculate Design. Review the calculated grade-beam depth and slab thickness. Round depths up to practical even inches if desired.
  6. Provide both top (negative) and bottom (positive) reinforcing in the grade beams plus continuous slab steel.
Key Formulas (Snowden / WRI TF 700-R-03)

Moment: M = w × Lc² × L′ / 2000  (kip-ft)

Beam depth: d ≈ (664 × M × Lc / B)1/3  where B = total width of all beams in that direction (in)

Cantilever length Lc derived from empirical (1-C) vs Ic curve and length-modification factors kL ≈ 0.93 / kS ≈ 0.80

Max beam spacing from experience-modified Fig. 17 of the WRI document

Relationship to the Attached Metal Building Drawings

The ACT Building Systems drawings (TALV1020879484) for the Hamlin / Mueller project show a ~60 ft × 30 ft main building plus lean-to. Column base reactions and anchor forces are given; the foundation itself is noted as “by others”.

This tool produces a conventional stiffened slab-on-ground that can support such light metal buildings on expansive Texas soils when the soils data are properly entered. Always coordinate anchor bolt layout, edge distance, and local thickening under columns with the metal building supplier’s reactions.

Metal Building Coordination Notes
  • Column base reactions and anchor quantities are shown on the ACT stamped plans — transfer those forces into the grade beams / thickened pads.
  • Edge rebate / thickened edge under wall panels is typically 1.5–2 in additional depth or a separate grade beam.
  • Keep diaphragm (roof & wall sheeting) continuous — do not cut for openings without engineering.
  • Finish floor elevation is shown as 100.00′ on the drawings; set top of slab accordingly.
